WHAT WAS FIXED
--------------
1) THE 3 "FAILED ANCHORS"  (were FALSE ALARMS)
   All three edits were ALREADY in your files; the patcher just counted
   "already applied" as a failure, and one anchor string had a literal \n
   that could never match. PATCH-v25.cjs now treats "replacement already
   present" as applied (⏭️) — re-run it and you get:
       already present: 18   failed anchors: 0

2) TT CMD PICKER GOES SILENT ON QUOTED REPLY
   The picker's in-memory store is wiped on every restart/reconnect, and all
   four "picker-first" gates required a LIVE store entry. With an empty store
   a quoted tap fell through to settings and died silently. Fix: when your
   reply QUOTES a picker/player card (detected by its prompt text), the quote
   alone is now enough — the picker is rebuilt from the quoted card
   (__ttRestoreFromQuote) and your number runs. No more silence.

3) "4 is not on that list" ON THE PLAYER CARD
   The quote-guard that is supposed to route a bare digit to the player (and
   away from the TikTok picker) only matched the literal text
   '𝑷𝑹𝑬𝑪𝑰𝑶𝑼𝑺 x PLAYER' / 'Reply here with a number'. The real card is
   '🎧 *BOTNAME — PLAYER*' (or 'JINX PLAYER') with prompt 'Reply with 1/2/3/4',
   so the guard never fired and '4' fell through to the TikTok picker, which
   rejected it. The guard now matches the actual card (PLAYER / 'Reply with
   1' / 'Reply here with a number'), so quoting the player card with 1-4 goes
   to the player and '4' = video.
